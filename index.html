The people
behind the screen
01

Ria

The Creative Mind

I'm Ria. The voice, vision, and occasional overthinker behind the screen. I collect what I'm learning, figuring out, and eating — giving heart and stories to the everyday moments that make life special.

I believe the little things often become the stories we remember most. Whether it's a new experience, a thought that stays with me, something worth sharing, or simply an ordinary day, I find joy in putting it into words. This space is where my thoughts find a home, one story at a time.

02

Surya

The Technical Mind

I'm Surya. I take care of the digital architecture, technology, and backend that bring this space to life.

While Ria fills these pages with stories, thoughts, and experiences, I work behind the scenes to build and maintain the space that brings them together. From the design to the little details that make the website work, I enjoy turning an idea into something people can experience.
