*I have a little theory: sometimes, we don't choose a song because of how it sounds. We choose it because of how it makes us feel.*

*The same song can mean something completely different to two people. One person might listen to it and think about someone they love, while another might find comfort in its melody or simply enjoy the memories it brings back.*

*For me, certain songs have a way of making everything feel a little lighter. They make me romanticise the smallest things, appreciate people a little more, revisit old memories and sometimes just sit quietly with my thoughts.*

*These are seven songs I keep coming back to. Not because they all tell some story, but because each one gives me a different reason to smile.*

**1. Aaja Piya Tohe Pyaar Doon – Lata Mangeshkar**

I think this song reminds me of the kind of love that doesn't need to be complicated.

There's something so beautiful about old Hindi melodies. The emotions feel sincere, the music feels effortless, and there's a certain innocence that you don't come across very often.

When I listen to this one, I think about the little ways love can be expressed. A thoughtful gesture, someone's presence, a warm conversation or simply making someone feel special without having to say much.

It makes me appreciate romance in its simplest form. Not the movie-like grand gestures, but the everyday kind that makes life feel a little sweeter.

And perhaps that's why this song feels so comforting. It takes me back to the idea that love can be soft, uncomplicated and beautiful.

**2. Mumma – Kailash Kher**

This one hits differently.

I think we all have moments when we get so caught up in growing up, working, chasing goals and figuring life out that we forget to pause and appreciate the people who have been there since the beginning.

When I listen to this song, I think of my mother and all the little things that often go unnoticed. The constant concern, the repeated questions, the reminders to eat, the care that sometimes comes disguised as scolding.

As we grow older, we start understanding the things we probably didn't pay much attention to as children.

And there's something emotional about realising that while we're busy trying to build our lives, someone is always quietly wishing the best for us.

This song makes me want to slow down, spend more time with my family and never take that kind of love for granted.

**3. Jo Tum Mere Ho – Anuv Jain**

This song is my little reminder to appreciate the feeling of being in love.

Not just the big moments, but the random ones. The conversations that go nowhere, the silly jokes, the comfortable silences, the unexpected smiles and the feeling of having someone you genuinely enjoy being around.

I like listening to this song and thinking about how lovely it is when a person becomes part of your everyday happiness.

It's also a reminder that love doesn't have to look perfect. It can be found in ordinary moments, in small efforts and in the comfort of simply being yourself around someone.

There's a softness to this song that makes me want to hold on to the good moments a little longer.

**4. Photograph – Ed Sheeran**

I'm a big believer in taking pictures.

Not necessarily because every picture has to be Instagram-worthy, but because I love the idea of being able to revisit a moment years later.

This song makes me think about all the versions of life we've lived so far. Childhood memories, family pictures, old friendships, holidays, celebrations and even those completely ordinary days that we didn't realise would become special.

Sometimes, you come across an old photograph and suddenly remember exactly how you felt that day, what everyone was wearing, what you were laughing about and how simple everything seemed.

I listen to this song with a little gratitude for the people and experiences that have shaped my life.

It reminds me to make memories while I'm busy living, because one day, these very moments might become the photographs I look back at.

**5. Tujhko Jo Paaya – Mohit Chauhan**

This song makes me think about happiness that comes from having the right people around you.

There's an excitement in it that I absolutely love. It reminds me of the feeling when someone makes your world a little brighter, when you look forward to meeting them, or when even a regular day becomes something you enjoy because they're a part of it.

I don't think we always realise how much the people around us influence our happiness.

Sometimes, it's a friend who makes you laugh until your stomach hurts. Sometimes, it's your partner, your family or someone who simply makes you feel understood.

This song makes me appreciate those connections.

It's one of those songs that can put a smile on your face without requiring any particular reason.

**6. Khat – Navjot Ahuja**

I think one of the most beautiful things about love is when someone pays attention.

Not just to the big things, but to the smallest details. Remembering something you casually mentioned weeks ago, knowing what makes you happy, noticing the little changes in your mood, or remembering the things you thought nobody was listening to.

That's what makes this song so special to me.

It makes me think about a kind of love where efforts come naturally. Where someone genuinely wants to know everything about you, remembers the little things, and makes you feel like you're an important part of their world.

And honestly, that's the kind of romance that melts my heart.

It's not always about expensive gifts or elaborate surprises. Sometimes, it's about remembering your favourite things, checking whether you've eaten, making plans around something you once said you wanted to do, or simply showing that they were listening.

I find something incredibly reassuring about being loved that thoughtfully. Because when someone remembers the little things, it tells you that they weren't just hearing you; they were paying attention.

And if you ask me, that's one of the sweetest ways to say, "You matter to me."

**7. Alag Aasmaan – Anuv Jain**

This one is for the part of me that is always dreaming about what's next.

I think there's a stage in life when you start realising how much there is still left to experience. Places to visit, things to learn, goals to achieve, people to meet and so many versions of yourself waiting to be discovered.

This song makes me think about possibilities.

It reminds me that life doesn't have to stay the same, that it's okay to want more, and that sometimes growing means being brave enough to imagine a different future.

I find comfort in that thought. We don't need to have every answer figured out. We can be happy with where we are while still looking forward to where we're going.

There's a certain hopefulness in this song that I really like.

**A little something before you go!**

I guess that's what I love about music. It gives us a reason to revisit memories, appreciate people, fall in love with ordinary moments and sometimes understand ourselves a little better.

And the best part? You don't need a particular mood or occasion to listen.

Some days, you'll want to hear an old melody. Some days, you'll want to think about someone special. And on other days, you'll just want to put on your headphones and forget about the world for a little while.

So, here's my little reminder: keep your favourite songs close, make time for the people you love, take more pictures, write things down and don't forget to enjoy the little moments.

> **Because sometimes, happiness is not a big event or a life-changing experience.**
>
> **Sometimes, it's just your favourite song playing at exactly the right time.**

With love,  
Ria ♡
