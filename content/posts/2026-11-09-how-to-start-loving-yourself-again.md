*There are phases in life when you realise you've been giving so much of yourself to everything and everyone that you've somehow forgotten what it feels like to show up for yourself.*

*Maybe you've been too hard on yourself. Maybe you've spent too much time trying to be enough for other people. Maybe life simply happened, and somewhere along the way, you stopped treating yourself with the same kindness you so easily give to others.*

*And then one day, you decide you want to feel like yourself again.*

*Not necessarily a completely different person. Just someone who feels a little more at peace, a little more confident, and a little more comfortable being who they are.*

*But where do you even begin?*

**1. Stop treating yourself like a problem that needs fixing.**

I think one of the biggest mistakes we make is believing that we need to become better, prettier, smarter, more successful or more confident before we deserve to feel good about ourselves.

We keep postponing self-love until some imaginary version of ourselves arrives.

But what if you don't need to fix everything about yourself to start being kinder to yourself?

You can want to improve your habits and still appreciate the person you are today. You can have goals and still be proud of how far you've come. You can acknowledge your flaws without making them your entire personality.

You are allowed to be a work in progress without treating yourself like unfinished business.

**2. Keep the little promises you make to yourself.**

Self-love isn't always about grand gestures or inspirational mornings.

Sometimes it's simply doing what you said you would do.

Getting enough sleep. Drinking water. Taking that walk. Finishing something you've been putting off. Saying no when you genuinely don't want to say yes.

Every time you keep a small promise to yourself, you build a little more trust in yourself.

And every time you don't, you don't need to punish yourself. You can simply begin again.

The goal isn't perfection. It's learning that you can depend on yourself.

**3. Stop making your life a competition.**

It's surprisingly easy to look at someone else's life and suddenly feel like yours isn't moving fast enough.

Someone is getting promoted. Someone is travelling. Someone is getting married. Someone seems to have figured out everything you are still trying to understand.

And suddenly, your own achievements feel smaller.

But you are comparing your entire life to carefully selected pieces of someone else's.

Your timeline doesn't need to resemble anybody else's to be meaningful.

You can celebrate someone else's happiness without using it as evidence that you're falling behind.

There is room for other people to shine without making yourself smaller.

**4. Learn to enjoy your own company.**

One of the nicest things you can do for yourself is create a life that feels good even when nobody else is around.

Take yourself somewhere you love. Listen to music while getting ready. Eat your favourite food without waiting for a special occasion. Read something that interests you. Go for a walk. Spend an evening doing absolutely nothing productive.

You don't need every moment to become a memory worth posting.

Sometimes, a good day is simply a day you enjoyed living.

And the more comfortable you become in your own company, the less you'll feel the need to constantly seek validation that you're doing life correctly.

**5. Get comfortable saying no.**

Not every opportunity deserves your yes. Not every person deserves unlimited access to your time. Not every expectation needs to become your responsibility.

You can be kind without being constantly available.

You can love someone and still disagree with them.

You can disappoint someone without being a bad person.

Boundaries aren't about becoming cold or selfish. They're about recognising that your energy, time and emotional wellbeing matter too.

And honestly, the people who genuinely care about you should be able to make room for your boundaries.

**6. Make room for things that make you feel alive.**

Sometimes we become so focused on becoming productive, successful or responsible that we forget to have fun.

When was the last time you did something simply because you enjoyed it?

Not because it would improve your career. Not because it would make you look interesting. Not because it was part of some self-improvement routine.

Just because you liked it.

Bring back the hobbies you abandoned. Try something new. Make playlists. Explore places. Spend time with people who make you laugh.

You don't have to earn enjoyment by being productive first.

**7. Give yourself permission to begin again.**

There will be days when you feel confident and days when you question everything.

Days when you feel like you've grown so much, and days when an old insecurity suddenly finds its way back.

That doesn't mean you've failed.

Growth isn't always a straight line. Sometimes it looks like recognising a pattern sooner than you used to. Sometimes it looks like walking away from something you once would have tolerated. Sometimes it looks like being able to forgive yourself for a mistake.

You don't have to restart your entire life because you had one difficult day.

You can simply continue.

A little reminder before you go.

If you're currently in a phase where you're trying to find yourself again, I hope you remember that you don't need to become someone extraordinary to deserve an extraordinary amount of kindness from yourself.

You don't need to have everything figured out.

You don't need to be everyone's favourite person.

You don't need to prove that you're worthy of the love you want.

Start with the way you speak to yourself. Start with the promises you keep. Start with the little things that make your days feel like yours again.

And when you forget, because you probably will sometimes, remind yourself gently.

> You are not someone you need to spend your entire life fighting against. You are someone you get to spend your entire life learning to understand.
>
> Maybe that's where loving yourself again begins.

**With love,**  
**Ria ♡**
