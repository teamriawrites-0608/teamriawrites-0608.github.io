*What if you could spend a day away from the city, experience a little village life, try your hand at traditional activities, swing under a tree, enjoy the monsoon and still have a whole lot of fun? That's pretty much what my visit to Monteria Village in Karjat was like.*

*I visited in August, and honestly, the weather couldn't have been better. Everything felt a little more special with the greenery, the cloudy skies and that lovely monsoon atmosphere. But the best part? There was so much to do, and I got to experience it all with the best company.*

Here's everything that made this visit worth remembering.

**1. August Weather? Couldn't Have Asked for Better.**

I genuinely think visiting Karjat during the monsoon makes the experience even better.

Everything around you looks greener and fresher.

The cloudy skies and cool breeze make being outdoors so much nicer.

The occasional rainfall adds to that proper monsoon getaway feeling.

And of course, the whole place looks beautiful in pictures.

It's one of those places where you actually want to spend time outside instead of sitting indoors.

**2. A Little Village Experience**

One of the things I really enjoyed was how Monteria Village brings together different elements of traditional Indian village life.

From village-style structures and little shops to spaces like the railway station, post office, Chaupal, Kutchi Bhunga and Anganwadi, there's quite a bit to explore.

What I liked was that it wasn't just about looking at things. You could walk around, explore different spaces and actually get a feel for the experience.

It was a nice change from the usual resorts and hotels we tend to visit.

**3. The Huts Have Their Own Charm**

The village-inspired huts are honestly one of the things that make the place feel different.

They're a lovely alternative to the usual hotel rooms, especially if you're looking for something a little more rustic and closer to nature.

Imagine waking up surrounded by greenery, with a completely different atmosphere from your everyday routine.

Even the stay itself feels like part of the experience.

**4. Tree Swings and the Simplest Kind of Happiness**

Okay, this deserves its own section.

There's something about swings tied to trees that just hits differently.

You don't need a fancy activity or an elaborate setup. Give me a swing, some greenery, good weather and the right people, and I'm already happy.

We spent time enjoying the little things, laughing, clicking pictures and just having fun.

And honestly, these are the moments that end up becoming your favourite memories.

**5. Trying Things You Don't Usually Try**

One of my favourite parts about places like Monteria is getting to experience activities that you wouldn't necessarily do in your everyday life.

Some of the traditional experiences include:

- **Khat weaving:** A chance to understand and try a traditional craft.
- **Pottery:** Getting your hands into clay and trying something creative.
- **Bamboo weaving:** Exploring a craft that requires patience and skill.
- **Blacksmithing and carpentry:** Seeing traditional skills in action.
- **Tailoring and farming activities:** A glimpse into everyday village occupations.
- **Tractor trolley rides:** Because sometimes even the simplest rides can be fun.

I really like the idea of experiences that let you do something rather than just watch something.

**6. And Then There's the Adventure Side**

If you're someone who gets bored sitting around, there's plenty to keep you occupied.

Depending on the activities available during your visit, you can explore things like:

- Giant swings and ziplining
- Zip cycling and rope courses
- Boating
- Swimming pool and rain dance
- Traditional games
- Anand Mela-style games like ring toss and glass toss
- Magic shows and puppet shows

There's a nice mix of activities, so the day doesn't have to revolve around just one thing.

You can go from trying something adventurous to playing a completely random game five minutes later.

**7. Good Food Makes Everything Better**

A day out is never complete without good food.

The day packages offer meals such as breakfast, lunch and hi-tea, depending on the package you choose.

And honestly, food tastes even better when you're eating with people you love spending time with.

The conversations, the random jokes and everyone talking at once — sometimes that's just as memorable as the activities themselves.

**8. The Best Company Makes All the Difference**

I think this is what made the entire experience so special.

Having people who are equally excited to try things, laugh at the most random moments, click pictures, enjoy the food and make the most out of the day makes such a difference.

Everything becomes more fun when you're sharing it with the right people.

And that's exactly how this day felt.

**Would I Go Again? Absolutely.**

Monteria Village was one of those experiences where there was something to enjoy at every turn.

The village atmosphere, the activities, the greenery, the swings, the food and, of course, the company.

It's a lovely option if you're looking to spend quality time away from the city without planning an elaborate holiday.

I'd especially recommend experiencing it during the monsoon, when everything around you feels so much greener.

And if I go back, I'm definitely spending more time on those swings.

> Because sometimes, the best days aren't necessarily the ones where you do something extraordinary.
>
> They're the ones where you enjoy absolutely everything about being there.

With love, Ria ♡
